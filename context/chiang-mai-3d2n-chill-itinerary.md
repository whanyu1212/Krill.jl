# Chiang Mai 3D2N Chill Itinerary (Date-Specific)
Base: 99 The Heritage Hotel, Chiang Mai Old City
Trip window: Fri 27 Mar 2026 (arrive around noon) -> Sun 29 Mar 2026 (depart 5:00 PM)

## Trip Style
- Pace: chill / low-intensity
- Structure: 2-3 highlights per day + hotel rest blocks
- Transport: walk where possible; Grab/songthaew for longer rides

## Pre-Trip Items
| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Light clothing + temple cover-up (shoulders/knees) | Temple dress compliance |
| Comfortable walking shoes/sandals | Old City is very walkable |
| Small cash notes (THB) | Markets, snacks, small shops |
| Sunblock, hat, water bottle | Midday heat |
| Grab app + offline Google Maps area | Smooth routing |

| 12:00-13:30 | Arrive in Chiang Mai + transfer to hotel + bag drop/check-in | Grab/airport taxi (~15-25 min) | Keep this window flexible for arrival delays |
